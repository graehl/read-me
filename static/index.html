Before you start

  • Use a quiet room. Hold the phone a hand's width to a forearm from your mouth.
  • Use the phone's own microphone or a wired one, not Bluetooth.
  • For each line: tap Record, read the line, tap Stop.

How to record

Setup

  • Use the phone's own microphone or a wired or USB one. A Bluetooth headset or a messaging app's voice path compresses the sound and removes detail the recognizer needs.
  • Normal speech should show clearly in the waveform without filling it. If some lines are shouted, record one of those first: the page tells you if it clipped.
  • Hold the microphone a hand's width to a forearm away, slightly to one side so breath does not hit it.
  • Use a quiet room with soft furnishings; avoid fans, traffic and echoey rooms such as bathrooms. Some quiet background sound is fine.

Each line

  • Read it as you would naturally say it in that style: plainly for formal lines, casually for spoken ones, with feeling for angry or rough ones. You do not need to act.
  • Read numbers, abbreviations and words from other languages the way you normally would.
  • If you stumble, tap Retry. Only the take you keep is used.
  • If a line has a typo or makes no sense, skip it (tap another line number at the top, or press →) and tell us its line number; do not correct it while reading.
  • Take breaks and record in several sessions if you like; your progress is saved. Keep the same microphone, room and distance throughout.
